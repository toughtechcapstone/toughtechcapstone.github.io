A pacemaker without surgery? USC Viterbi’s Qifa Zhou and team's ultrasound breakthrough could change heart care - USC Viterbi | School of Engineering

viterbischool.usc.edu

I wanted to share this USC Viterbi article about a new approach to cardiac pacing that uses ultrasound and gene therapy to potentially eliminate the need for surgical implantation.

What caught my attention was the tradeoff behind the innovation. While it could make cardiac pacing less invasive, it also introduces new safety and regulatory challenges. It made me wonder whether patients and physicians would embrace this approach when traditional pacemakers are already effective, and what it would take to make the technology commercially viable.
