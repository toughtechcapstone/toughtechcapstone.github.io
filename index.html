ARPA-H selects teams to make made-to-order genetic medicine manufacturing possible | ARPA-H

arpa-h.gov

I wanted to share this article: ARPA-H selects teams to make made-to-order genetic medicine manufacturing possible

What stood out to me is that developing effective genetic medicines is only part of the challenge. Manufacturing individualized treatments affordably, consistently and close to patients may be just as difficult. ARPA-H's investment is an interesting example of how government funding could help de-risk the manufacturing and quality control infrastructure needed before personalized therapies become commercially scalable.

* Gladys

Re:Build, which spun out Slate Auto, raises $2.1B | TechCrunch

techcrunch.com

Re:Build, the Massachusetts manufacturing accelerator that spun out Slate Auto, just raised $2.1B led by General Catalyst and JPMorganChase to build more hard tech products and launch new industrial startups in the US. I found it really interesting because it shows capital moving toward the factory side of tough tech, which makes me wonder how much of the commercialization gap for our capstone technologies is about manufacturing rather than the science itself.

— Michelle

This startup's self-driving excavators help dig up $600M in new funding | TechCrunch

techcrunch.com

Sharing this TechCrunch article about Bedrock Robotics, a startup that recently raised $600 million to expand its autonomous excavator technology.

What caught my attention is that the company has moved beyond pilot testing and is now using its technology on actual construction sites. While this is a major step toward commercialization, I'm curious how the company will scale its technology across different construction environments while maintaining safety and reliability.

— Pattarasaya

Musely secures $360M from General Catalyst without giving up equity | TechCrunch

techcrunch.com

Read how Musely, a profitable dermatology telehealth platform, landed $360M in non-dilutive funding from General Catalyst through a revenue-share model instead of a traditional VC round.

— Monica

Sunlight, water and a new, cleaner way to make green hydrogen | Cyprus Mail

cyprus-mail.com

After nearly four years, the EU FreeHydroCells team has a solar water-splitting cell that needs no external power, and it is only at TRL 4. What stands between them and TRL 5 is durability, larger cell areas, cheaper materials and manufacturing-compatible processes, which says a lot about where solar fuels really get stuck.

— Jack

A YC startup is putting $1M behind 5 unsolved materials science problems for AI | TechCrunch

techcrunch.com

Discovered Materials, a months-old San Francisco startup using AI to find materials for advanced chips, is offering prizes for AI that solves five open problems defined with academics, including a Stanford professor. The co-founder’s point is that materials science is harder to benchmark than math, where answers are clear-cut.

— Charlene
