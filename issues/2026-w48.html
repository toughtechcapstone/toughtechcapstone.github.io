Non-contact sensor detects wound infections before symptoms appear: Querrey Simpson Institute for Regenerative Engineering - Northwestern University

renu.northwestern.edu

I wanted to share this article: Non-contact sensor detects wound infections before symptoms appear Northwestern University

What caught my attention is that researchers developed a wireless sensor capable of detecting early signs of wound infection by analyzing gases released from the skin, without disturbing the healing tissue. Beyond the technology itself, I'm interested in whether continuous monitoring could reduce preventable complications and unnecessary clinic visits enough to justify the cost of implementing these devices in routine wound care.

Sharing this Fierce Biotech article about Astellas' potential $1.3 billion partnership with Audax Biosciences to develop a new cancer immunotherapy.

The technology is still in preclinical development, but it has already attracted interest from a major pharmaceutical company. I found this interesting because it shows how early-stage research can create business value through partnerships and licensing, even before clinical validation. It also raises the question of how companies value technologies with such high scientific uncertainty.
