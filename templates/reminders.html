What this course is trying to do

You are the business lead for a real invention from a UCLA or Southern California lab, working alongside its technical team. Tough tech ventures face five core risks: technology, market, regulatory, scale, and financing. In the fall you learn each one and scope the problem with your PI. In the winter you go deep on the one that matters most right now, gather real evidence, and turn it into a lab-to-market plan.

You don’t need to be a scientist or engineer. Technology is only one piece of building a tough tech startup.
